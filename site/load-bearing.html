Load-bearing gems

A gem matters to the ecosystem by how many gems pull it in transitively — directly, or as a dependency of a dependency, turtles all the way down. If Spinel can't compile a load-bearing gem, nothing above it can ship either. This is the build-it-first roadmap.

110.3kdoesn't compile

~29.1k gems compile on their own but can't actually be used because something beneath them is rejected. Fixing a load-bearing blocker flows up the tree — its dependents become buildable too.
