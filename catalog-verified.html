★ verified — 141 gems

Full surface compiles and a behaviour smoke matches CRuby under a Spinel-compiled harness — every lib/ file force-required (no autoload masking, no missing-dependency rescue), not just the entrypoint. The only verdict to trust where it matters. A constant/VERSION-only smoke that loads the entrypoint but leaves the gem's real code behind autoload is not enough — that overstated usability, so the bar was tightened to whole-surface. Sticky across engine revisions until a re-run catches a regression. Or: for a gem the mechanical probe can't rank — a Spinel-native program rather than a require-library (e.g. tep, the translator that compiles this very site) — a 👤 human attestation of real production use is the verification (the strongest signal we carry); a fresh behaviour failure still overrides it.
