57af7f9 → cb23cc6 ↑ 4.4k graduated ↓ 715 regressed

Mutex/Thread come in from the cold. With matz/spinel#1360 landing (Mutex#synchronize/Thread.new now run single-threaded and carry their block's value), the static pre-filter no longer hard-rejects them — they're flagged risky (correct for defensive use, degenerate only for true concurrency). Re-probing the 8,833 gems that had been rejected on sight for Mutex.new/Thread.new, 3,493 (39.5%) moved out of rejected — they compile now; the static wall had been hiding it. The four spine gems (bundler/rake/minitest/thor) now reject for their real reason — the deep metaprogramming surface (send/method_missing/cattr_accessor) — not a misleading hard:Mutex.new. The differential re-audit also caught one regression in the wave: returning self from a reopened-builtin method (#1386, to-bool), filed same day.

cb23cc6 → b60fbd7 ↑ 51.1k graduated ↓ 1.7k regressed

The largest movement in the catalog's history. 636 upstream commits — a type-inference rewrite that resolved the entire unresolved:<method> reject family (~60k records → ~0): ~49,000 gems moved out of rejected (−46%), +22k to clean. The wave also changed unresolvable require to hard-fail the compile (post-#1383); because require "gem/version" is near-universal that spuriously rejected thousands, so the probe now classifies a require-only failure as the no-load-path limit (risky [load-path:require], 9,716 gems corrected). Cost side, caught by re-verifying every ★ at this rev: ~34 of 175 behaviour-verified gems regressed (12 miscompiles incl. after_commit_action, 22 codegen incl. bare-super/ForwardingSuperNode) — the trust tier dropped 175→136. Fresh-verify overrode stickiness, so the ★ count is honest, not carried.

The clean-up wave. matz closed every bug this harness filed in the b60fbd7 absorption — within a day, each by a named commit. Net: +4,061 gems to clean (79.7k → 83.7k), rejected 57.7k → 58.4k roughly flat. ★ 136 → 141 (4 cleanly restored: bundler_signature_check, hudson, pry-plus, logger_pipe; the rest of the b60fbd7 regressions were load-path-entangled, not the bugs that got fixed).
